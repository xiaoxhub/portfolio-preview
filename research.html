Work About Research Résumé

Understanding user behavior, across very different users

From open government data users to older adults facing scams, I ask the same questions: what people are trying to do, where they get stuck, and what helps. The same methods drive my product work.

How this shows up in product work

CHI 2022 Extended Abstracts Segmenting users by what blocks them

I grouped open data users by their challenges and proficiency. InLicense uses the same kind of split: an experienced BD and a new BD need different help.

See it in the InLicense case study →
ASIS&T 2020 What people do, not only what they say

Transaction logs showed how people actually reached a local open data portal. ExRxAI builds on the same idea: the tablet counts reps instead of asking patients to report them, and the physician’s workbench flags patients by what their training records show.

See it in the ExRxAI case study →
ACM CHIIR 2019 · Best Short Paper Research that ends in a design

The paper identified why people struggle to access open datasets and proposed a data guide to support them. DART courses followed the same loop, revised after each pilot with older adults.

See it in the DART Academy case study →
Computers in Human Behavior · in press A product designed and tested with its users
